# Alool Technologies Website

Build a complete, production-oriented marketing and portfolio website from the supplied interactive prototype. Preserve the approved Alool positioning and visual system: architectural grids, strong typography, restrained motion, proof-first editorial layouts, the Alool Weave, modest radii, and borders instead of decorative shadows.

The audience is organizational decision-makers evaluating software engineering and digital transformation partners. The experience should feel clear, grounded, capable, and regionally rooted without presenting Alool as a generic agency, support firm, outsourcing company, or AI company.

Content must not invent clients, logos, testimonials, metrics, awards, partnerships, or outcomes. Representative work and pending legal/contact content must be labeled clearly.

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, semantic HTML, and responsive behavior at 1440, 1024, 768, and 390 pixels.
