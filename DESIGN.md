# Alool Technologies Design System

## Reference

The supplied `/Users/dhaban/Downloads/alool_agency_prototype.html` is the primary visual and interaction specification.

## Direction

Premium agency/editorial composition expressed through restrained Swiss functionalism and a warm, regionally grounded character. Use a strict grid, visible rules, purposeful green, large typography, deliberate light/dark transitions, and the Alool Weave or system orbit to visualize connected systems.

## Color

- Ink: `#101412`
- Warm White: `#F4F3EE`
- Stone: `#D8D7D0`
- Graphite: `#555B57`
- Alool Green: `#176B52`
- Signal Green: `#2B8E6E`

## Typography

Space Grotesk for display and Inter for body and UI, or the closest production-safe equivalent when font files are unavailable. Oversized, tight display type should carry the hierarchy.

## Layout

Mobile-first four-column logic expanding to a 12-column desktop grid. Use editorial spacing, thin structural borders, large media areas, modest radii, and minimal shadows. Interior pages must share the same visual grammar as the homepage.

## Motion

Use short opacity, trace, and transform reveals only. No bounce, perpetual motion, scroll hijacking, or cursor gimmicks. Remove non-essential motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, full keyboard access, visible focus, semantic structure, sufficient contrast, touch targets of at least 44 pixels, 200 percent zoom resilience, and reduced-motion support.
